# Sources and original interpretation
Verified 2026-10-11.
- [Square Enix's official FFX/X-2 manual index](https://support.na.square-enix.com/manual.php?id=9681) links [English manual](https://cdn.sqexeu.com/sea/ffxii/manuals/FFX-FFX2_PS3_ManInt_English_013014.pdf). Printed pages 9–11 confirm command-only CTB, visible order and recovery changes, in-battle switching, unique roles and Overdrive charging. Pages 16–18 confirm connected adjacent node activation. This is the primary mechanic reference.
- [Battling guide](https://www.hxchector.com/final-fantasy-x-hd-remaster-walkthrough/battling/) supplements element behavior and weakness information. The game's own Ember/Tide/Volt multipliers and creature traits are original rules, not exact FFX formulas.

Glasswake borrows strategic patterns: conditional initiative, visible recoveries, distinct party roles, reserves, elemental matching, charged bursts, connected progression and a journey. It simplifies to four heroes, five chapters, three elements, a shared seven-node constellation, two statuses, and checkpoint-only saves. Voluntary swaps transfer the current action opportunity for free, so the incoming keeper acts immediately; the following command consumes its own recovery (previous earned recovery remains actor-owned, while opening initiative is not debt); individual charge and a shared Lighthouse replace exact Overdrive/summon rules. No copied cast, maps, locations, dialogue, soundtrack, models or artwork. Art is authored SVG; music is a short original sine-wave motif.
