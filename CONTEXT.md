# Glasswake language
**Keeper**: One of four traveling lighthouse guardians.
**Active keeper**: A keeper occupying one of three battle slots.
**Reserve**: A living keeper outside those slots.
**Tick**: A unit of conditional initiative, never wall-clock time.
**Delay**: Recovery ticks an action adds before an actor can act again.
**Charge**: Individual stored power gained from actions and incoming damage.
**Lighthouse**: Collective surge earned through successful elemental weakness hits.
**Spark**: Shared progression currency awarded to the whole party.
**Constellation**: Connected permanent upgrade nodes.
**Checkpoint**: Journey progress immediately before an encounter, restored on defeat or reload.
